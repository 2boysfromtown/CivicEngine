The Civic Grievance Bottleneck

  • Language Barriers: Traditional portals require text entry in standard English/Hindi, excluding millions of regional-language speakers.
  • Mismatched Ward Routing: General citizen complaints route to centralized queues instead of local ward officers, leading to massive SLA delays.
  • Redundant Duplicates: Dozens of citizens report the same pothole or heap, causing administrative noise and database bloat.
  • Lack of Real-world Action: Delayed complaints rotting in lists leave citizens powerless without a legal follow-up receipt.
🚨 The Citizen Slop

Clunky text forms, manual drop-down ward selections, and no voice-based assistance lead to drop-offs.

📉 Operational Overhead

Ward officers are flooded with identical duplicate reports, requiring manual review and sorting.

Gemini Live Voice Assistant

Precise Location Routing

  • Local Boundaries cache: Point-in-polygon mapping of coordinates against DataMeet shape files for India.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass Boundary Resolution: Fallback query resolving administrative boundaries at admin_level=10 (Ward level in Indian Municipalities).
  • Google Maps Geocoding API: Last-resort fallback for neighborhoods and sublocalities.
  • Geohash Indexing: Real-time Firestore geohashes for high-performance spatial querying.

Semantic Duplicate Prevention

  • Ward Pre-Filtering: Fetches active issues in the same category & ward first, avoiding expensive global database queries.
  • AI Similarity Checker: Gemini reviews active complaints and compares location details to detect matches.
  • Auto-Upvote Conversion: If matched, the new report is merged. The original issue is automatically upvoted and re-ranked.
  • Real-time UX Redirect: Citizen gets an overlay: "Duplicate report resolved! Upvoted existing case Reference: CE/..."
✅ Operational Efficiency

Instead of 50 separate tickets for one open manhole, authorities get one ticket with 50 upvotes, pushing it instantly to the top of the urgency queue.

RTI Act 2005 Legal Integration

  • Citizen Legal Power: Pre-filled RTI application drafts are generated for overdue or unresolved complaints.
  • Section 6(1) Pre-formatting: Automates standard Indian RTI structures, referencing Public Information Officer (PIO) offices.
  • Pre-populated Case Metrics: Inserts GPS coordinates, category, reported date, tracking ID, and delay timeline logs.
  • Direct Receipt Download: Compiles a ready-to-print text receipt document in one click.

• Database: Google Cloud Firestore (asia-south1 region)
• Storage & Auth: Firebase Client/Admin Auth, Google Cloud Storage
